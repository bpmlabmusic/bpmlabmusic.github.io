Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BPM Lab Music ("we", "us"), the independent music project behind the YouTube channel @BPMLabMusic and the website bpmlabmusic.com, handles information. It also covers BPM Lab Publisher, the internal tool we use to publish videos to our own YouTube channel.

The short version: we do not sell data, we do not run ads or trackers on this website, and our publishing tool only works with our own channel.

1. This website

  • The website is a static site with no user accounts, no forms, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, for security and operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request (including your IP address) to Google. See the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Our YouTube channel

When you watch our videos, comment, or subscribe, you are using YouTube, and your data is handled by Google under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. As a channel owner we only see the aggregated statistics that YouTube Studio provides to every creator, plus public comments. We do not receive your personal account details.

3. BPM Lab Publisher and the YouTube API Services

BPM Lab Publisher uses YouTube API Services. It is an internal tool that is not offered to the public: it is authorized only by the Google account that owns the BPM Lab Music channel, and it acts only on that channel.

What it does. It uploads our own workout videos and sets their details on our channel: titles, descriptions and translations, captions, playlists and scheduled publish times.

What data it accesses. Only the data of our own channel that is needed for those tasks: the channel's ID and name, and the IDs and details of our own videos and playlists. It does not access, collect, store or share information about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How the data is stored. The OAuth authorization token issued by Google is stored only on our own computer. Data returned by the API is used only while a publishing job runs and is not kept beyond what is needed; any stored API data is deleted or refreshed within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies.

Sharing. We do not sell, rent or share any data obtained through the YouTube API Services with third parties, and we use no advertising or analytics services in the tool.

Cookies. BPM Lab Publisher runs on our own computer and does not place cookies or similar technologies on anyone's device.

Revoking access. The authorizing account can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the tool immediately; we then delete the stored token.

By using any part of our services that relies on YouTube, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. When you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

5. Children

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Because this website collects no personal data, there is usually nothing for us to hold. For anything we may have from an email, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
